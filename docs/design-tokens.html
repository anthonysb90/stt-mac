Design tokens, before the views

Every view in Aloud pulls from this file. No component defines its own colour, size, radius, duration, or spacing value. If a view needs something the system does not have, the token is added here first.

  • — colour tokens
  • — type steps
  • — spacing steps
  • 2 appearances, defined together

An AppKit app has no stylesheet. Without a deliberate system the values scatter across a dozen controllers and light/dark parity rots within a week.

Naming them once makes the design reviewable as a design, and makes “tighten every gap by 2pt” a one-line change instead of an archaeology project.

Colour

Semantic, not literal. A view asks for TEXT_SECONDARY, never #585868. Each token carries both appearances, so switching is automatic and cannot be forgotten. Chips below show light on the left, dark on the right — always both, regardless of how you are reading this page.

Type

macOS-native and short. 13pt is the system control size; everything else is a ratio away from it. A scale with eleven steps is a scale nobody follows. Specimens below are set in the real system face at the real size.

Spacing

A 4pt grid, with 2pt and 6pt half-steps below 8 for optical adjustment inside controls. Bars are drawn at true width.

SPACE

The raw scale. Every margin and gap in the app is one of these numbers.

INSET

Compositions of the scale, so “the padding inside a card” is one decision made once. A component may not invent its own.

Radius & border

Drawn at true size. md is 6 because that is the macOS control radius — matching it is what keeps buttons from looking foreign.

RADIUS

sm for chips and the meter, md for controls, lg for cards and rows, xl for sheets and the window.

BORDER

Weights in points. The focus ring is drawn outside the control bounds.

Shadow

Three elevations plus flat, and most things are flat. Dark mode carries a heavier alpha at every level, because a shadow that reads on white is invisible on #1B1B20.

SHADOW

Offset and blur in points; alpha differs per appearance. What you see below is the value for the appearance you are reading in.

Motion

Nothing on a desktop app should exceed slow. Views check accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion and fall back to instant.

DURATION · EASING

Each track runs its real duration on the standard curve. Watch them side by side — the difference between 120ms and 200ms is the whole argument for having a scale.

METER_ATTACK · METER_RELEASE

The level meter gets its own ballistics, and they are asymmetric on purpose: it has to jump to a new peak immediately to feel connected to your voice, but fall slowly or it strobes.

Metrics

Sizes that are neither spacing nor type but still must not be invented per view — control and row heights, sidebar and window geometry, icon sizes.
